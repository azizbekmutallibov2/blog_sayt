- Dark canvas #151619; paper #1e1f23; text #ededf0; neutral accent #dedfe5 and highlight #2b2d34. Dark mode uses soft graphite surfaces without the violet tint.

## Review

The visual identity depends on the author and notebook composition, with a vertical navigation rail and a large split lead story. Repeated two-column image cards and the prior top logo/More composition have been replaced. Violet is a restrained stationery accent; the large opening words carry the emphasis. No fake career claims, decorative sequence numbers or synthetic testimonials. Text remains left aligned and readable. All navigation is visible directly, with native horizontal scrolling on narrow screens.
